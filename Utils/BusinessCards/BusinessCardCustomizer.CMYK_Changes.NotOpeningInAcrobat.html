Business Card Editor Help

Selecting Elements

Click a shape in the preview, or a row in the SVG Elements tree, to select it.

Moving Elements

Drag a selected element with the mouse, or use the arrow keys. Hold Shift while dragging to move only vertically, or Alt to move only horizontally. Arrow keys move by 1 unit; hold Shift for 10 units, or Alt for 0.1 units.

Zooming & Panning

Scroll the mouse wheel over the image to zoom, or use the +/− buttons. Hold Shift while zooming to step by 10% instead of the standard zoom levels. Click the zoom percentage to reset to 100%. Tap the space bar or click the hand button to toggle the panning tool, then drag to pan around the image.

Visibility

Use the eye button to hide or show the selected element.

Dev Mode

Switch to Dev Mode with the toggle button in the floating toolbar at the bottom-right corner to open the SVG Elements tree, view element details, and control whether only movable elements can be selected.
